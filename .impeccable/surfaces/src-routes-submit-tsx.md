# Add event

Visitor mode: Operate

Audience: a parent or youth leader on a phone posting one game or a run of them. Job: get the dates on the fridge without retyping the whole form.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Adding dates is stacking more ruled notes on the same pad — not a spreadsheet, not a second form, not a shared location that later gets overridden.

OWN-WORLD: Same fridge: enamel, magnet red, marker blue, sticky yellow, underlined fields, marker-hand section titles. Each date is its own pad slip: when and where live together on that slip.

STORY: You fill the event once. One date is the default. Multiple dates peels the when/where into stacked slips. Each new slip copies the last time and place so a tournament is mostly tapping Add another date. Who to call stays one contact for the whole post.

FIRST VIEWPORT: “Add event” in marker red. Title and youth names. Then When: a date, starts, optional ends, and a Multiple dates marker link beside the heading. Place and address sit under that single date until Multiple dates is on; then they move onto each slip. Sticky yellow posts all of them.

FORM: Fridge Calendar, inherited from home. Seed key c66c702d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: tapping Multiple dates splits the single when/where into dated slips; adding another date copies the previous slip’s times and place onto a new ruled block.
