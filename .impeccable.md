## Design Context

### Users

Parents and families in Celina Ward, mostly on phones, checking what youth games, concerts, and activities happen this week. Secondary job: post an event quickly. Mental state is hurried and practical (carpool, gym, foyer)—not exploring a product.

### Brand Personality

Warm, clear, neighborly, with youthful energy. Voice of a well-printed ward bulletin that still feels like games and concerts, not a meeting. Helpful, specific, never corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction

Light by default; follow `prefers-color-scheme` for dark. Mix paper-bulletin warmth with varsity/concert energy. Anti-references: generic dark glass dashboards, Eventbrite card stacks, neon-on-black “AI SaaS,” and the current zinc radial-gradient shell. Theme and identity follow the phone and the ward, not a template.

### Design Principles

1. This week is obvious in the first viewport.
2. Density of a family fridge calendar, not a marketing landing page.
3. One primary action in reach: Add event.
4. Color and type carry warmth and energy; chrome stays quiet.
5. Every control works with a thumb; copy fits a narrow screen.
